NewsDesk

Editorial, calm, a warm serif accent — the design system behind a personalized news feed.

Voice

Calm and editorial, not flashy. A warm cream paper ground, hairline borders instead of shadows, a serif display face for headlines paired with a clean grotesque for everything functional. The one accent color (clay) is used sparingly — for the italic half of the wordmark, category labels, and active/selected states — so it keeps its signal value.

Color

Pulled directly from the app's tokens (src/index.css). ink-900 is the only primary text color; weight and the serif face carry the rest of the hierarchy. clay-500 is reserved for exactly four things — the wordmark's italic half, category labels, selected/active states, and the card hover border — so a new use of it should be checked against those first.

Spacing

A 4px-based scale. Chips sit 8px apart; card padding and the rhythm between stacked fieldsets is 16px; a popover or sheet gets the most room at 20px.

Radius

Radius communicates hierarchy: small for content (almost square, editorial), pill exclusively for interactive controls, and the largest steps reserved for popovers and sheets so a floating panel always reads as "above" the grid.

Components

Three recurring pieces cover the current UI.

Button

A solid ink-900 pill for the one primary action in a context. The header's "Personalize feed" trigger uses the pill; the inline "Add" confirmation currently uses radius-md instead — a real inconsistency in the app, shown here as-is.

Chip

The one interactive-choice shape. Drives preference toggles, applied filter chips (with a remove affordance), and the "+ Add" trigger that opens a picker.

Card

The article card every result renders in — a plain link styled as a bordered rectangle, no shadow. Hover swaps the border to clay-500.
